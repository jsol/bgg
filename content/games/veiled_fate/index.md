9 movable workers (demigods) on a circular map, each player secretly roots for
a specific demigod and wins if that demigod collects the most points. For more
players than 5 then two players might root for the same demigod and wins
together. Figuring out which player roots for which demigod is not necessary,
but it does help. Most points after three rounds win.
